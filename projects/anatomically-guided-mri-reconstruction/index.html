Anatomically Guided Reconstruction for Deuterium Metabolic Imaging

June 2023 · Python, sigpy, matplotlib

Implemented structure-guided total variation regularized image reconstruction with fast gradient projection for deuterium metabolic imaging scan enhancement, as a research experience for undergraduates in the Radiological Sciences Laboratory.

Presented results as a digital poster at the 2024 annual meeting of the International Society for Magnetic Resonance in Medicine in Singapore.
